The Last-Click Illusion

Last-Click Attribution

Google Analytics, platform pixel dashboards, and standard UTM tracking.

Credits the Cash Register: Awards 100% of revenue credit to brand search and retargeting ads shown to buyers already holding their cards.

Upper-Funnel Blind Spot: Connected TV, YouTube, and brand awareness get zero credit because customers rarely click immediately.

Blinded by Privacy Shifts: Degraded by iOS updates, cookie restrictions, and cross-device buyer journeys.

Creates Boardroom Conflict: Finance sees rising blended CAC while platform dashboards report fictional 8x returns.

Cadence: Real-time, but misleading.
The 90-Day Spreadsheet Lag

Legacy Consulting MMM

Traditional marketing mix modeling agencies and static quarterly reports.

Obsolete on Arrival: Takes 3–6 months to deliver an agency slide deck long after market conditions have shifted.

Synthetic Assumptions: Relies on artificial priors and manual adjustments that cannot be audited by finance.

Zero Operational Agility: Provides abstract formulas with zero weekly tactical guidance for media pacing.

High Consulting Retainers: $250k–$500k annually for backward-looking reports that gather dust.

Cadence: Quarterly or annual post-mortems.
